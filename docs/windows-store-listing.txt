PRODUCT NAME
================================================================================
Eupub

================================================================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION
================================================================================
Read EPUB and PDF books in euspell, a reformed English spelling that is
predictable enough to sound out. Switch between reformed and original spelling
as you read. Everything happens on your own computer.

================================================================================
DESCRIPTION
================================================================================
English spelling records history rather than pronunciation. You have to know a
word already in order to say it, which is why "though", "through", "thought",
"tough", "cough" and "bough" share four letters and almost nothing else. The
cost falls hardest on people learning English and on readers who find reading
difficult.

Euspell is a reformed spelling designed to be predictable. Eupub is how you read
real books in it.

Open a book and turn the reform on: "the people thought they could read through
the rough night" becomes "the peeple thoht they coodd read thruh the ruff niht".
Turn it off and the original spelling returns, in the same place on the same
page. Nothing is permanent, and nothing stops you switching back mid-sentence.

WHAT IT READS
- EPUB books
- PDF documents, reformed in place so the page keeps its original layout,
  figures and fonts
- Plain text files

WHILE YOU READ
- Turn euspell on or off at any point, keeping your position
- Bookmarks, highlights, and search inside the book
- Adjustable text size, and a light or dark page
- Your place is remembered, and the last book reopens when you return

A PDF opens in reformed spelling and is navigated by its contents; bookmarks,
highlights and search are for EPUB and text books.

HOW THE REFORM IS APPLIED
A 205,000-word lexicon is built into the app, together with a classifier that
resolves words whose spelling depends on how they are used - so "records" the
noun and "records" the verb are told apart and spelled differently, which a
table of rules cannot do.

ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
Eupub has no account, no sign-in and no server. It makes no network requests at
all: no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising. The lexicon travels inside the
app, every conversion happens on your machine, and no book you open is ever
uploaded. You can use it with the network switched off.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Eupub is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later licence. The source, including
the lexicon and the conversion engine, is public at github.com/ossiak/eupub.

A short sample book is built in, so there is something to read the moment you
open it.
