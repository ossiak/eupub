Eupub is an offline e-reader that displays EPUB and PDF books in "euspell", a
reformed English spelling, and lets the reader switch between reformed and
original spelling at any point in the text.

1. DEMONSTRATION VIDEO
A screen recording made on a physical iPhone is attached to our reply in App
Store Connect. It begins with launching the app and shows the core loop: the
bundled sample book opening by itself, the euspell toggle switching the same
page between reformed and original spelling, page navigation, font size and
light/dark, and a PDF opened through the document picker. The app has no
account registration or login, no purchases or subscriptions, no
user-generated content, and requests no permissions, so none of those flows
appear.

2. DEVICES AND OS VERSIONS TESTED
Tested on physical iPhone 14, iOS 26.0, simulator iPhone 17, iOS 26.5

3. WHAT THE APP DOES, AND FOR WHOM
English spelling records history rather than pronunciation: a reader must
already know a word in order to say it. That falls hardest on people learning
English and on readers with reading difficulties. Euspell is a systematic
respelling that makes the written form predictable, and Eupub is how a reader
tries it on real books rather than on a table of rules: open a book, turn the
reform on, and read.

The audience is adult general readers - people learning English, readers with
dyslexia or similar difficulties, and anyone curious about spelling reform.
There is no age-restricted content of any kind; the app displays whatever book
the user opens.

Conversion is performed by a 205,000-word lexicon bundled inside the app,
together with a classifier that resolves words whose spelling depends on
context. Nothing about the text leaves the device.

4. SETTING UP AND REACHING THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, no login, no configuration. There are no credentials to provide
because there is nothing to sign in to.

A sample book is built in. On first launch the app opens it automatically, so
there is content with no setup at all. The "euspell" toggle in the reader
chrome switches that text between reformed and original spelling; this is the
app's central feature and needs nothing but the app itself.

To open your own book, tap "Open" and choose an .epub, .pdf or .txt file
through the standard document picker (iCloud Drive and other providers are
reachable from there). Files can also be placed in the app's Documents folder,
which appears as "On My iPhone > Eupub" in the Files app.

To exercise PDF support, open any PDF through "Open". The PDF is rendered
normally and its text layer is reformed in place, so the page keeps its
original layout and graphics. We are glad to supply a sample PDF at a URL if
that would help.

Please note one intentional difference: a PDF is always displayed in reformed
spelling. The conversion is applied once as the document is rendered, so the
"euspell" toggle is deliberately disabled while a PDF is open, and appears
greyed out. It is not an unresponsive control - it applies to EPUB and text
books, where switching back and forth is possible. We plan to extend it to PDFs
in a later version.

The app is iPhone-only and portrait-only, by design: it is a single-column
reader sized for phone width.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. The app makes no network requests and has no server, no backend, no
analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It uses no data provider,
authentication service, payment processor or AI service.

Everything needed for conversion ships inside the app: the lexicon is a
read-only SQLite database in the bundle, queried locally through the system
libsqlite3. This is also why the submission declares
ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false - there is no network traffic to protect.
The app can be used in airplane mode from first launch onwards, which is the
simplest way to confirm this.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app behaves identically in every region and on every storefront. It
has no region-specific content, pricing, features or restrictions, and does not
detect or respond to location: it requests no location permission and has no
network access with which to infer one. The interface and the reform it
implements are English-only, which is a property of the subject matter rather
than a regional variation.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is in no regulated industry and provides no regulated service.

Eupub contains no third-party copyrighted books or texts. The bundled sample
was written for the app. Every other book is one the user supplies from their
own device or storage, and nothing is distributed with the app.
