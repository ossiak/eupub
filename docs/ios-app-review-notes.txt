Eupub is an offline e-reader that displays EPUB and PDF books in "euspell", a
reformed English spelling, and lets the reader switch between reformed and
original spelling at any point in the text.

WHAT THE APP DOES, AND FOR WHOM
English spelling records history rather than pronunciation: a reader must
already know a word in order to say it. That falls hardest on people learning
English and on readers with reading difficulties. Euspell is a systematic
respelling that makes the written form predictable, and Eupub is how a reader
tries it on real books rather than on a table of rules.

The audience is adult general readers - people learning English, readers with
dyslexia or similar difficulties, and anyone curious about spelling reform.
There is no age-restricted content of any kind; the app displays whatever book
the user opens. Conversion is performed by a 205,000-word lexicon bundled
inside the app, with a classifier for words whose spelling depends on context.
Nothing about the text leaves the device.

SETTING UP AND REACHING THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, no login, no configuration, no credentials - there is nothing to
sign in to. The app has no purchases or subscriptions, no user-generated
content, and requests no permissions.

A sample book is built in. On first launch the app opens it automatically, so
there is content with no setup at all. The "euspell" toggle in the reader
chrome switches that text between reformed and original spelling; this is the
app's central feature and needs nothing but the app itself.

To open your own book, tap "Open" and choose an .epub, .pdf or .txt file
through the standard document picker. Files can also be placed in the app's
Documents folder, which appears as "On My iPhone > Eupub" in the Files app. To
exercise PDF support, open any PDF through "Open": it is rendered normally and
its text layer is reformed in place, so the page keeps its original layout.

One intentional difference: a PDF is always displayed in reformed spelling. The
conversion is applied once as the document is rendered, so the "euspell" toggle
is deliberately disabled while a PDF is open and appears greyed out. It is not
an unresponsive control - it applies to EPUB and text books. The app is
iPhone-only and portrait-only by design: a single-column reader sized for phone
width.

EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. The app makes no network requests and has no server, no backend, no
analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It uses no data provider,
authentication service, payment processor or AI service. The lexicon is a
read-only SQLite database in the bundle, queried locally through the system
libsqlite3. This is also why the submission declares
ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false: there is no network traffic to protect.
The app can be used in airplane mode from first launch, which is the simplest
way to confirm this.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app behaves identically in every region and on every storefront, with
no region-specific content, pricing, features or restrictions. It does not
detect or respond to location: it requests no location permission and has no
network access with which to infer one. The interface and the reform are
English-only, which is a property of the subject matter rather than a regional
variation.
